## Voltstar

The 35-page entered order contains a deletion duty in Section 11. After privileged material is identified and notice is received, the receiving party must return the material or confirm steps to permanently delete its electronic copies from electronic records. Section 13 governs inspection and controlled copies of source code. Section 15 requires return or destruction of protected material after the designated request. These provisions were checked against the codebook's `DB` rule, which requires an express obligation associated with a named database, electronic review room, litigation-support platform or comparably specific storage system.

The source search and relevant page checks located no obligation to delete from such a named system. The original `DB=0` conditional judgment remains supported under that narrower code definition. This is a scoped, researcher-conducted negative review, not an external second-reader or a visual inspection of every page. General electronic deletion remains recorded under its applicable codes.
